### 3. Trend Signal Analysis
**Explosive Attention on "Agentic Skills" & The MCP Ecosystem**
The most explosive signal today is the massive influx of traction toward "Skills"—curated instructions and capabilities directly injected into CLI assistants. Projects like `garrytan/gstack` and `mukul975/Anthropic-Cybersecurity-Skills` prove the community is moving past building monolithic models and is instead focusing on standardizing how AI agents interact with development environments and complex frameworks (like MITRE and NIST). Alongside this, MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers like `DeusData/codebase-memory-mcp` are becoming foundational infrastructure. The AI stack is rapidly standardizing around LLM + MCP + Skills.

**The Rise of Multi-Agent Orchestration over "Prompt Engineering"**
We are seeing a distinct shift toward multi-agent frameworks and "harnesses" (`deer-flow`, `ECC`, `NousResearch/hermes-agent`) that handle long-horizon tasks autonomously. Developers are no longer just tweaking prompts; they are building orchestration layers that give agents memory, sandboxes, and tool-execution capabilities to perform tasks over hours rather than single turns. 

**Direct Connection to Frontier Releases (GPT-5.6 & Claude Code)**
Today's trends are highly reactive to the latest proprietary releases. The appearance of `Awesome-GPT-5.6-API-and-Prompts` indicates a recent OpenAI model drop, driving massive developer experimentation. Similarly, a huge portion of today's trending infrastructure (`mattpocock/skills`, `gstack`, `claude-mem`) is explicitly built as extensions for Anthropic's "Claude Code" CLI. The open-source community is effectively building an open ecosystem of plugins to enhance proprietary frontier models.
