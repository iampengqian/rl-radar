### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current Hugging Face landscape signals an absolute triumph for **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** architectures alongside a strong pivot toward **multimodal generation**. Families like DeepSeek-V4, Qwen3.6, Nemotron-3-Ultra, and JetBrains' Mellum2 are heavily relying on MoE to provide massive total parameter counts while keeping active compute remarkably low. Open-weight models are clearly dominating the frontier, outpacing proprietary API-only releases in raw community engagement and downloads. 

Furthermore, **quantization is no longer an afterthought but a first-class citizen**, with official and community groups (like Unsloth) releasing GGUF, FP8, and NVFP4 versions concurrently with base launches. A notable micro-trend is the rise of "Uncensored" and "Aggressive" community fine-tunes (e.g., HauhauCS) which consistently pull millions of downloads. Finally, Nvidia's Cosmos3 and LocateAnything suggest a major industry pivot toward "Physical AI" and spatial intelligence, while models like Sulphur-2 bring high-end text-to-video generation (LTX-2.3) directly to the open-source community.
