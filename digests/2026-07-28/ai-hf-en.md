### 3. Ecosystem Signal
**The Qwen3.5 and 3.6 MoE family is undeniably the backbone of the current open-source ecosystem**, serving as the foundational architecture for nearly all top-performing fine-tunes, uncensored models, and specialized coding variants. There is a strong ecosystem trend toward **open-weights over proprietary APIs**, led aggressively by Asian tech giants (Baidu, Moonshot, Alibaba/Qwen, ZAI). 

Furthermore, **extreme quantization is rapidly maturing**. The presence of 1-bit and 2-bit GGUF models (like Prism-ml's Bonsai series) and NVFP4 formats (Poolside, Baseten) proves that users are demanding hardware-accessible formats to run 27B–250B parameter models locally. Finally, specialized utility models—particularly OCR (Baidu, ATH-MaaS) and local Edge TTS (Inflect)—are generating massive download volumes, indicating that the community is looking for highly targeted, production-ready tools rather than just generalist chatbots.
