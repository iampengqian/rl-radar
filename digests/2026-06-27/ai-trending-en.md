### 3. Trend Signal Analysis
Based on today's trending data, the open-source community is experiencing an explosive shift toward **Agent Standardization and Adversarial Workflows**. The most highly starred tools today (like `design.md`, `gstack`, and `learn-claude-code`) aren't just general frameworks; they are specific "harnesses" and "skills" built around coding agents like Claude Code. Developers are treating agent configuration files as first-class citizens in the dev stack, enforcing strict rules, design systems, and structured prompts (MCP) to make LLMs act predictably.

A fascinating new direction is the emergence of **Web-Scraping as an MCP Standard**. With tools like `Agent-Reach` gaining massive traction (+1,164 stars today), the community is clearly rejecting expensive API tiers in favor of headless browser automation that gives agents raw, free access to platforms like Reddit, Twitter, and Bilibili.

Finally, there is a distinct connection between recent LLM releases and domain-specific multi-agent systems. The fact that `ai-berkshire` (AI value investing) and `TauricResearch/TradingAgents` are surging shows that frontier models (like GPT-5.6 or Claude's latest updates) are finally capable of maintaining distinct personas (e.g., mimicking Buffett vs. Munger) and engaging in adversarial debate to produce high-level financial analysis.
