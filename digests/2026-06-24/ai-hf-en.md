### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem reveals a massive surge in Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures and unified multimodal models. Open-weight titans like DeepSeek-V4-Pro, GLM-5.2, and Google's Gemma-4 are dominating the landscape, proving that proprietary APIs are facing stiff competition from open-access release strategies. Qwen 3.5/3.6 has firmly established itself as the absolute backbone of the community fine-tuning scene, particularly for vision-language and agentic models.

Furthermore, local deployment is scaling up rapidly. As flagship models grow in capability, the community is aggressively generating GGUF and FP8 quantizations to run them locally, with a specific focus on coding, reasoning, and removing alignment constraints (evidenced by the millions of downloads on uncensored/abliterated models). Specialized, hyper-optimized architectures for RAG (FastContext, LiquidAI's ColBERT) and real-time audio/OCR are also maturing, signaling that enterprise use-cases are moving heavily into open-source adoption.
