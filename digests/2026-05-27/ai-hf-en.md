### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The AI open-weight community is currently experiencing a "Qwen3.6 Summer," with Alibaba's Qwen3.6 series completely dominating the daily download charts across multiple parameter sizes. We are seeing a decisive shift toward Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, perfectly illustrated by the massive adoption of the 35B-A3B models, which offer heavy-weight performance at a fraction of the active parameter compute cost. 

Quantization remains king for practical deployment. Unsurprisingly, 'unsloth' is leading the pack with highly compressed GGUF formats that allow users to run 27B and 35B models locally. Furthermore, "uncensored" and "obliterated" variants are seeing disproportionately high download numbers compared to their base model counterparts, indicating strong enterprise and hobbyist demand for models stripped of corporate safety RLHF. Finally, the generative space is aggressively pivoting from static images to temporal media—text-to-video, audio-to-video, and voice cloning models are surging, filling out the open-source creator toolkit.
