### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem is defined by three major forces: the **Qwen3.6 dominance**, the **push for extreme quantization**, and the **appetite for uncensored local AI**. 

Qwen has effectively captured the open-weight MoE space, with Qwen3.6 variants generating massive download volumes (over 8.5 million combined). Interestingly, the community is actively choosing to strip alignment from these models (e.g., "Uncensored," "Heretic," "Aggressive"), indicating strong grassroots demand for completely unfiltered base models for creative and specialized use cases. 

Technologically, sub-4-bit quantization has reached a turning point. The success of Prism-ml's 1-bit and 2-bit "Bonsai" models, alongside NVFP4 formats, proves that massive 27B-35B parameter MoEs can now run efficiently on consumer edge hardware without catastrophic quality loss. Meanwhile, large tech players (Google, Baidu, Nvidia) are dominating specialized pipelines—specifically scaling multimodal OCR, lightweight ASR, and edge robotics—which highlights a broader industry pivot toward complex, multimodal physical-world AI applications.
