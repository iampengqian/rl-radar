### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The ecosystem is experiencing a decisive shift towards native multimodality and Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures. Model families like Qwen (3.5/3.6), Gemma 4, and GLM 5.2 are dominating the charts, indicating massive momentum for open-weight MoE designs that balance high capability with lower inference costs. Open-weight releases from major labs (DeepSeek, Google, Nvidia) continue to crush proprietary APIs in raw community adoption, as seen by multi-million download volumes in just a week.

Simultaneously, local deployment is scaling rapidly. The community is pushing massive downloads of aggressive fine-tunes and GGUF quantizations (e.g., HauhauCS, DavidAU, yuxinlu1). This proves that high-parameter MoEs are successfully trickling down to consumer hardware via llama.cpp. There is also a noticeable trend towards "uncensored" and reasoning-focused ("Thinking") local models, highlighting a community demand for highly autonomous, unfiltered, and capable coding and agentic tools. Finally, edge computing is diversifying beyond text, with notable peaks in streaming ASR and ultra-small TTS architectures.
