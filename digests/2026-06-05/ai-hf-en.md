### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current trending landscape heavily favors **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** architectures. Rather than simply scaling up dense parameters, families like Qwen3.6 (activating 3B out of 35B) and DeepSeek-V4 are dominating download charts by offering immense capacity with highly constrained compute requirements. 

There is also a distinct shift toward **open-weight multimodal supremacy**. Google’s Gemma-4 is explicitly tagged as "any-to-any," and top-tier models from Step and Qwen are increasingly blurring the line between pure text generation and vision-language tasks. 

Furthermore, **quantization is no longer a community-only afterthought**; major players like Nvidia (NVFP4) and Ideogram (fp8/nf4) are releasing officially compressed models directly alongside their standard weights. Finally, the ecosystem signals a massive demand for **uncensored, locally executable models**. Community fine-tunes like the "HauhauCS-Aggressive" MoE are racking up millions of downloads, proving that users want capable, unrestricted models that can run completely offline via tools like Unsloth and llama.cpp.
