### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The June 2026 leaderboard clearly signals that the era of massive, dense models is yielding to extreme **sparsity and MoE architectures**. Users are enthusiastically adopting models that punch above their parameter count, proven by the massive download volumes for DeepSeek V4 Flash, LiquidAI's highly sparse LFM2.5, and various Qwen MoE variants. **Nvidia’s proprietary hardware quantization formats** (NVFP4) are also taking a strong hold, allowing titanic 550B models and heavy video generators to run efficiently. Open-weight models continue to rule the download charts, though community "uncensored" GGUF fine-tunes by creators like HauhauCS show a relentless appetite for unmoderated, localized LLMs. Meanwhile, **generation is going omnimodal**: we are seeing a rapid convergence of text, audio, and video, with video avatar generation and real-time streaming voice models carving out their own prominent, permanent categories on the hub.
