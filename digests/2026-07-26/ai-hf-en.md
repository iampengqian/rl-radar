### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem is defined by two primary forces: **MoE Multimodality** and **Extreme Quantization**. Qwen has firmly established its 3.5/3.6 MoE architecture as the foundational substrate for the community, serving as the base for everything from vision-language models to autonomous computer-use agents. Concurrently, Google's Gemma 4 proves that massive download metrics are achievable for open-weight models that bridge the gap between proprietary scale and open accessibility. 

Furthermore, the open-weight community is pushing hardware boundaries further than ever. The proliferation of NVFP4 formats (optimized for newer NVIDIA architectures) and 1-bit/2-bit ternary quantizations (like the Bonsai models pulling millions of downloads) proves that users prioritize extreme footprint reduction to run 27B-35B models locally. The emergence of Vision-Language-Action (VLA) pipelines for robotics on the trending board also signals that physical AI is transitioning from closed labs to open-source frameworks.
