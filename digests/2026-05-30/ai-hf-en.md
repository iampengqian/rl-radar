### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The open-source AI ecosystem is currently defined by the rapid proliferation of **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** and **Vision-Language Models (VLMs)**. The Qwen 3.6 architecture (both 27B dense and 35B-A3B MoE) has effectively conquered the community fine-tuning space, mirroring the historical dominance of Llama models. DeepSeek's V4 series proves that open-weights can still rival proprietary scale, while MoE architectures (like LiquidAI's LFM2.5 and Tencent's Hy-MT2) are becoming the default method for deploying heavy parameter counts without prohibitive compute costs. 

Furthermore, **quantization is no longer just a niche community effort**; it is a primary distribution channel. Unsloth’s GGUF releases are pulling nearly a million downloads weekly, proving that accessibility on consumer hardware drives massive adoption. Finally, specialized generative media is diversifying beyond static images into complex, multi-modal pipelines. ByteDance's "any-to-any" Lance model, SulphurAI's text-to-video engine, and ComfyUI-compatible node models (like circlestone-labs/Anima) indicate a maturing demand for holistic generative workflows.
