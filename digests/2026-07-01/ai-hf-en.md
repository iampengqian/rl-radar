### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The mid-2026 AI landscape shows a clear maturation of Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, with the Qwen 3.5/3.6 and GLM-5.2 families establishing total dominance over the open-weight LLM space. We are seeing a massive shift from standard chatbots toward "agentic" and "world-model" frameworks. Proprietary models face stiff competition not just from raw open weights, but from a highly optimized deployment ecosystem. Hardware-efficient quantization is the defining meta-trend: NVIDIA's NVFP4 format and Unsloth's GGUF conversions are capturing millions of downloads by solving the VRAM bottleneck. Furthermore, "uncensored" and "abliterated" community fine-tunes are seeing explosive, multi-million download growth, proving a sustained market demand for unrestricted, locally-hosted models.
