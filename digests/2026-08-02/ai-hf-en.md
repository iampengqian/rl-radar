### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The August 2026 data reveals a clear pivot: **multimodality is now the default**. Image-text-to-text pipelines are no longer niche, dominating both the top likes (Kimi-K3) and pure utility downloads (Baidu's Unlimited-OCR). 

In terms of model families, **Qwen (versions 3.5 and 3.6)** has firmly established itself as the open-weight foundational champion, acting as the base for almost all trending community fine-tunes. Interestingly, the open-source community is heavily leaning into "uncensored" and "aggressive" MoE blends (seen in the massive download numbers for DavidAU and HauhauCS), proving a relentless demand for unaligned creative models. 

On the quantization front, standard 4-bit and 8-bit formats are being challenged by ultra-low bit solutions. The presence of 2-bit ternary models (Ternary-Bonsai) and NVFP4 quantizations indicates that the ecosystem is aggressively optimizing for memory bandwidth, pushing to run 30B-250B parameter models on consumer and edge hardware without sacrificing conversational fidelity.
