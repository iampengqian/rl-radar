### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current Hugging Face landscape is defined by the rapid convergence of modalities and an overwhelming preference for open-weights. Google’s Gemma 4 and DeepSeek’s V4 series are proving that open (or open-weight) models can rival proprietary APIs, driving millions of downloads in mere days. The Qwen 3.6 family (particularly its MoE variants) is dominating the ecosystem, acting as the foundational architecture for a massive wave of community fine-tunes and quantizations. 

Unsurprisingly, local deployment readiness is a massive trend. Quantized formats like GGUF are seeing hundreds of thousands of downloads, heavily propelled by ecosystem enablers like Unsloth. Alongside text, generative media is exploding. Text-to-video (Sulphur-2), 3D asset creation (Pixal3D), and highly realistic voice cloning (Dramabox) are drawing immense community praise, indicating a shift where creators and developers are building full-scale multimedia pipelines entirely from open-source tools.
