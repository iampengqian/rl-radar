### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem highlights a definitive transition to Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures as the new standard, driven by the massive adoption of the **Qwen3.6** family (over 5.1 million downloads) and **DeepSeek-V4**. Open-weight models are rapidly cannibalizing proprietary API usage by successfully matching frontier capabilities while offering unmatched versatility. A standout trend is the massive consumer demand for local execution; the Qwen3.6-35B "Uncensored/Aggressive" fine-tune alone has generated nearly 4 million downloads, proving a strong market for unaligned, locally run models.

Furthermore, "any-to-any" unified architectures like Google's **Gemma-4** and **DiffusionGemma** indicate a shift away from task-specific pipelines toward holistic multimodal frameworks. Consequently, optimization libraries and quantizers—particularly those outputting **GGUF** formats (by Unsloth, yuxinlu1, and HauhauCS)—are acting as the primary bridge between massive enterprise-grade models and local consumer hardware.
