### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem reveals a massive shift toward **agentic, reasoning, and hardware-optimized deployments**. The Qwen3.5/3.6 ecosystem is dominating traffic, particularly MoE architectures (like the A3B variants), driven heavily by community demand for local deployment via GGUFs and aggressive uncensoring. Meanwhile, Nvidia’s strategic release of NVFP4-optimized models indicates a strong push to standardize low-bit quantization directly on the GPU manufacturer's terms, capturing millions of downloads in the process. 

On the base model front, DeepSeek-V4 and GLM-5.2 are firmly establishing themselves as the new heavyweight champions of open-weights. There is also a clear trend of "task-specific efficiency"; rather than just scaling up parameters, organizations like Baidu (OCR), Microsoft (Context/Agents), and Nvidia (LocateAnything, ASR) are releasing highly capable 3B to 4B models tailored for specific pipelines, proving that the meta is moving from general-purpose chat to specialized, deployable micro-services.
