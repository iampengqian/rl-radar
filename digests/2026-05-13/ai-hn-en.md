### 3. Community Sentiment Signal
Today’s HN AI discourse is defined by a stark contrast between hyper-technical pragmatism and deep institutional skepticism. The most active topics revolve around the Musk/Altman trial and the tragic ChatGPT overdose lawsuit, revealing a community highly critical of AI leadership ethics and deeply concerned about the unchecked real-world harms of consumer-facing AI hallucinations. 

There is a clear consensus that current AI agents are fundamentally brittle and need traditional software engineering paradigms applied to them—evidenced by the massive popularity of Statewright and LLM guardrails. Developers are pivoting away from relying solely on prompt engineering, leaning instead into deterministic tooling (state machines, cost trackers, token reducers) to make AI usable and safe. 

Compared to previous cycles dominated by hype over raw model capabilities, today's focus has shifted noticeably toward *reliability, accountability, and friction*. The Ask HN thread questioning whether AI is actually making engineering worse perfectly encapsulates this mood: a community experiencing AI fatigue, demanding practical safety and efficiency over grandiose promises.
