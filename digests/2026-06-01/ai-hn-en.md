### 3. Community Sentiment Signal
Today's HN community exhibits a highly pragmatic, slightly skeptical stance toward AI, focusing heavily on the chasm between theoretical capability and operational reality. The most active discussions center not on model marvels, but on deployment friction—specifically, the high cost of AI bills (prompting the Netflix tool open-source) and the realization that building software is no longer the primary hurdle for startups (scoring 46 points and 26 comments). 

A clear point of controversy is bubbling up around AI-generated code in critical open-source infrastructure. The rsync project's bold move to ban LLM commits suggests a growing fatigue among veteran maintainers who fear hallucinated code will introduce subtle vulnerabilities. Meanwhile, the engineering conversation has definitively shifted from "which model is best?" to "how do we build the UI, terminal managers, and Git workflows necessary to control these agents?" Overall, the hype cycle has cooled on this front page, replaced by a builders' focus on infrastructure, FinOps, and quality assurance.
