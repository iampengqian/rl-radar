### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem trends reveal a massive transition toward **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** and **multimodal architectures** as baseline standards, rather than novelties. Major Chinese AI labs (DeepSeek, Baidu, Moonshot) are dominating the open-weight proprietary release space, offering state-of-the-art capabilities that rival western closed models. 

Concurrently, the open-source community is aggressively pivoting to **Qwen 3.5 and 3.6** as the foundational backbones of choice for fine-tuning, with a distinct, massive appetite for "uncensored" and "agentic/computer-use" capabilities. The download metrics heavily favor models that provide hardware optimization: Unsloth’s GGUF conversions and Nota-AI's NVFP4 quantizations prove that enabling local, consumer-grade execution is the primary driver of actual model usage. Furthermore, edge-AI is finally branching out cleanly from text, evidenced by the high engagement in local CPU-bound TTS (Inflect-Micro-v2) and BitNet ASR models.
