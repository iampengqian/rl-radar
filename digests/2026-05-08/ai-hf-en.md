## 3. Ecosystem Signal
The May 2026 ecosystem is currently defined by the explosive synergy between frontier open-weight releases and highly specialized community quantization. **Qwen3.6** and **Gemma 4** are the undisputed momentum leaders; Gemma 4 dominates raw download volume (surpassing 8.5M for the 31B-it variant), while Qwen’s MoE architecture (35B-A3B) is the preferred target for community modifications. 

Open-weight models are clearly cannibalizing proprietary attention spans, driven significantly by the "de-alignment" trend: uncensored fine-tunes like `Gemma-4-31B-JANG_4M-CRACK` and `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-Uncensored` sit comfortably in the top ranks for likes and downloads. Furthermore, **Unsloth** continues to be the absolute backbone of the open-weight ecosystem, as their GGUF formats account for multiple millions of downloads, proving that local, offline inference via tools like llama.cpp is now a baseline expectation for developers.
