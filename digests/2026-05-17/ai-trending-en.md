## 3. Trend Signal Analysis
Today's GitHub data points to a defining paradigm shift: **the transition from monolithic LLMs to modular "Agentic Skills" and local "Super Intelligence" apps.** Projects like *OpenHuman* (+1,601 stars) and *Superpowers* (+1,281 stars) dominate today's traffic, proving that developers are moving past basic chat interfaces. They are now focused on building highly contextual, autonomous developer tools and localized AI companions that prioritize absolute privacy and zero-latency execution. 

A prominent new technical direction is the shift from traditional vector databases to **code knowledge graphs and "vectorless" RAG**. Tools like *Codegraph* and *PageIndex* are solving the token-bloat and hallucination issues typical of legacy RAG systems. This indicates an industry-wide maturation in how we manage AI context—favoring graph-based reasoning and structured retrieval over pure semantic similarity. 

This trend connects directly to the recent release of advanced reasoning models (e.g., Kimi-K2.5, GLM-5) and the widespread adoption of CLI agents like Claude Code. As foundational models become more capable of complex reasoning, the open-source community is rapidly building the middleware (memory layers, knowledge graphs, skill frameworks) required to effectively harness this intelligence in real-world, multi-step workflows.

## 4. Community Hot Spots
*   **The Rise of CLI Agent Ecosystems:** Projects like `everything-claude-code` (184k stars) and `superpowers` indicate that developer tooling is heavily shifting toward terminal-based AI assistants. Expect a massive influx of "Skills" and "Instincts" repositories for coding agents.
*   **On-Device AI Over Web-Apps:** With `OpenHuman` and `Supertonic` trending simultaneously, there is a clear, growing appetite for local-first, privacy-preserving AI applications that run natively via ONNX or Rust without needing cloud APIs.
*   **Spatial & Non-Visual AI:** `RuView`'s ability to use standard WiFi signals for spatial intelligence and vital sign monitoring is a breakthrough. It represents a major opportunity for hardware hackers and privacy-focused smart home developers to bypass cameras entirely.
*   **Vectorless / Graph-based RAG:** Keep an eye on the transition away from pure vector search. `PageIndex` and `Graphify` highlight that hybrid or graph-based retrieval offers significantly better context for complex codebases and document structures.
