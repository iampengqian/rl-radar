### 3. Ecosystem Signal
**Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) has completely solidified as the industry standard for 2026.** The top trending models—DeepSeek-V4, Qwen3.6, Liquid LFM2.5, Tencent Hy-MT2, and Keye-VL—all rely heavily on sparse MoE architectures, allowing massive total parameter counts (up to 35B) while maintaining remarkably low compute footprints (e.g., 1B to 3B active parameters). 

In the model family wars, **Qwen3.6 is the undeniable king of the open-weight ecosystem**, dominating both the official leaderboards and community fine-tuning charts. Hubs are exploding with Qwen3.6 quantizations (GGUF, NVFP4) and niche fine-tunes, showing a vibrant ecosystem similar to early LLaMA waves. Consequently, the hardware utilization race is on: NVIDIA is actively pushing its own `NVFP4` quantization standard directly into the open-source community, while edge initiatives like `llama.cpp` and extreme ternary/1.58-bit quantization (e.g., prism-ml) are aggressively optimizing these massive models for local consumer hardware.
