### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem reveals a massive bifurcation between heavyweight foundation models and hyper-optimized local deployments. The **MiniMax-H3** release has effectively synchronized the community, with ComfyUI nodes, quantized NVFP4/INT4 versions, and LoRA adapters dominating the top charts. This indicates a huge appetite for local, node-based video generation workflows. 

Simultaneously, the open-weight LLM space is aggressively iterating, moving entirely past 2024's dense model dominance into Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) territory, as seen in GLM-5.2 and the Qwen3.6 lineage. Community fine-tunes are heavily leaning towards "uncensored" and highly specific local formats (GGUF), proving that power users want modular control over their models. Interestingly, practical utility models like Baidu's Unlimited-OCR are generating massive download numbers (nearly 3 million), showing that solving specific, painful enterprise problems yields incredible organic traction alongside flashy generative releases.
