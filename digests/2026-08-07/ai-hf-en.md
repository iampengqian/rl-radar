### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The ecosystem is experiencing two massive, parallel shifts. First, **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) has become the absolute standard**, with top-tier LLMs (GLM-5.2, DeepSeek-V4, EXAONE 2.0, Qwen3.6) and multimodal models all shifting to sparse architectures (e.g., 750B total parameters with only 37B active). This allows frontier-level performance at significantly reduced inference costs. Second, **multimodal video generation is being heavily democratized.** The release of MiniMax-H3 has sparked a flurry of community activity mirroring the early Stable Diffusion era—developers are rapidly producing LoRAs, ComfyUI wrappers, and mixed-precision formats (NVFP4, INT8) to run massive video models on consumer GPUs.

Furthermore, **Chinese tech labs are dominating the open-weight frontier.** Models from DeepSeek, MoonShot, Baidu, Zhipu AI (GLM), and Alibaba (Qwen) consistently outpace Western counterparts (except for specialized tools like Mistral's Shieldstral and Microsoft's Mage-VL) in raw engagement and download volume. Finally, the **"uncensored" GGUF community** remains voracious, with modders like DavidAU stripping safety alignments from Qwen3.6 models to generate millions of downloads from local AI enthusiasts who prioritize privacy and content freedom.
