### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The ecosystem is currently experiencing a definitive shift toward **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** and **extreme quantization**. Model families like Qwen (3.5/3.6), Gemma-4, and DeepSeek-V4 are utterly dominating the charts. We are seeing a clear trend where the community expects baseline models to be multimodal ("any-to-any"), as seen with Gemma-4 and MiniMax-M3. 

Open-weight models are unequivocally winning the developer mindshare over proprietary APIs, largely due to the maturity of the quantization ecosystem. The staggering download numbers for NVFP4 and GGUF formats—spearheaded by optimizers like Nvidia, Unsloth, and HauhauCS—indicate that users want frontier-class reasoning locally. Furthermore, the appetite for "uncensored" and "agentic" fine-tunes shows a developer base focused on building autonomous, unfiltered workflows for coding and cybersecurity, rather than just conversational chatbots.
