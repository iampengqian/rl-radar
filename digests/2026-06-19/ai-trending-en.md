### 3. Trend Signal Analysis
The most explosive trend today is the massive capitalization of **Agentic Engineering** and **Agent Memory/Harnessing**. Tools like `obra/superpowers` and `Kilo-Org/kilocode` are gaining thousands of stars daily, proving the community is moving past conversational AI toward fully autonomous software development pipelines. 

A clear new technical direction is the rise of **MCP (Model Context Protocol) servers** and **Codebase Knowledge Graphs**. `DeusData/codebase-memory-mcp` proves that developers are incredibly focused on solving the "context window bottleneck." Instead of just appending text to prompts, the ecosystem is demanding sub-millisecond indexing to feed structured graph data into models, reportedly cutting token usage by up to 99%. Additionally, we see the emergence of "hypergraph" data extraction (`Hyper-Extract`) and high-performance, in-process vector databases (`alibaba/zvec`), signaling a need for hyper-local, embedded AI memory.

These GitHub trends map directly to the recent LLM releases. The explicit mentions of GLM-5 and models like Claude Code and Codex show the industry is reacting to frontier models capable of deep tool use. The ecosystem is frantically building the scaffolding—context layers, token optimizers, and skill frameworks—to maximize the utility of these highly capable, tool-native LLMs.
