### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The ecosystem is currently defined by two parallel movements: the rapid maturation of massive proprietary-grade open-weights, and an insatiable community demand for local, uncensored variants. Model families like Qwen (specifically the 3.5/3.6 MoE architectures) and DeepSeek-V4 are forming the backbone of the hub's activity. However, the raw foundation models are taking a back seat to community adaptations—DavidAU and LuffyTheFox's "Uncensored" and "Heretic" GGUF finetunes are pulling massive multi-million download numbers, proving that local, unrestricted alignment remains a primary use case for open-source developers. 

Simultaneously, Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) has become the absolute standard architecture for new releases, visible in K-EXAONE, GLM-5.2, and Qwen derivatives. Alongside LLMs, practical multimodality is hitting an inflection point; Baidu's Unlimited-OCR and Microsoft's Mage-VL are being adopted at enterprise scale, while the community eagerly awaits video generation integrations (like the MiniMax-H3 ComfyUI nodes) to mature.
