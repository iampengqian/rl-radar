### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The ecosystem is currently experiencing a **Qwen3.6 Renaissance**. Qwen's MoE architecture (specifically the 35B-A3B variant) has entirely captivated the open-weight community, serving as the base for nearly every major uncensored, vision, and code fine-tune on the leaderboard. 

Concurrently, **ultra-low bit quantization is reaching mainstream maturity**. We are seeing a shift away from standard 4-bit GGUFs toward experimental, highly compressed formats like 2-bit ternary weights (`prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai`) and Nvidia's NVFP4 format (`nota-ai/Solar-Open2-250B-Nota-NVFP4`). This allows massive models (up to 250B parameters) to be run locally or on edge devices. 

There is also a clear pivot toward **agentic and multimodal utility**. Microsoft's introduction of `computer-use` and BitNet ASR, alongside highly specialized OCR models from Baidu, indicates that the community is moving beyond pure chatbots toward models that can actively see, read, and interact with operating systems and documents.
