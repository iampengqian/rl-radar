### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem is heavily defined by the transition to highly efficient Sparse Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architectures, notably driven by the Qwen 3.5/3.6 series and DeepSeek-V4. The "A3B" naming convention across multiple fine-tunes indicates a strong appetite for models with billions of parameters that maintain a highly active 3B parameter footprint during inference. 

Open-weight dominance is at an all-time high, with proprietary labs like Microsoft (Mage-VL, Fara1.5) and Baidu (Unlimited-OCR) contributing heavily to open repositories. However, the most striking trend is the sheer volume of downloads driven by community fine-tunes. "Uncensored" GGUF releases by creators like DavidAU and HauhauCS are racking up millions of downloads, proving that local, unrestricted deployments are a primary use case for the Hugging Face community. Quantization (via Unsloth, GGUF, and NVFP4) remains the critical bridge connecting these massive flagship models to consumer hardware.
