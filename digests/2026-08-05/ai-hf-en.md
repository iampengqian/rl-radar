### 3. Ecosystem Signal
The current ecosystem is heavily defined by the triumph of **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** and aggressive **model merging**, largely spearheaded by the Qwen 3.5 and 3.6 architectures. The download charts prove that open-weight models are dominating the space, particularly due to the immediate availability of community-driven quantizations (GGUF, NVFP4) by creators like *unsloth* and *DavidAU*. 

There is a striking ecosystem trend toward **"uncensored" and "Heretic" fine-tunes**, indicating a strong developer appetite for models stripped of corporate safety guardrails for creative and unrestricted use. Furthermore, frontier modalities are becoming incredibly accessible: Baidu’s *Unlimited-OCR* proves that high-volume document processing is fully open-sourced, while the hype around *MiniMax-H3* highlights text-to-video as the next major battleground. Overall, open-weight innovation is moving faster than ever, driven by efficient quantization techniques that allow massive MoE models to run directly on consumer hardware.
